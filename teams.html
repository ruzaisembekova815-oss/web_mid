Eleven teams, two cars each. Here is how a team works and who builds the engines.

How a team is built

A Formula 1 team is called a constructor because it builds its own car. Every team enters two cars, so every team has two race drivers. Drivers score points for themselves, and both cars score points for the team, so there are two championships.

Behind every driver there are hundreds of people: engineers, strategists and mechanics who work at the factory and at the track.

  • Team principalRuns the team and makes the big decisions
  • DriverDrives the car and gives feedback to the engineers
  • Race engineerTalks to the driver by radio during the race
  • StrategistDecides when to make pit stops
