How a race weekend works

A Grand Prix is not one race but three days. Drivers practise on Friday, fight for their starting positions on Saturday and race on Sunday. The faster a driver is in qualifying, the closer to the front they start.

Some weekends have a shorter sprint race, so the schedule can change. Check the official calendar before you watch.

  • FridayPractice: teams test the car and tyres
  • SaturdayQualifying: sets the starting grid
  • SundayThe race: the full distance for points

How points work

Only the first ten drivers in a race score points. Points are added up over the season, and the driver and the team with the most points win the championships. Sprint races give points separately.
